# Grades of Climbing Converter - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 17, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Android app "Grades of Climbing Converter", also published as "Grades of Climbing - Converter" (package `com.etcho.grades_of_climbing_converter`, the "App"), processes, why, and what rights you have.

## Summary

- **You do not need an account.** The order of the scales, your favorites and your settings stay on your device.
- The App shows a banner ad from **Google AdMob**. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and similar), we ask for your consent first.
- The App uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to learn which parts of the App are used and to fix crashes. They receive device and usage information.
- We never sell your data.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

- **Your arrangement:** the order in which you placed the grading scales and which ones you marked as favorites.
- **Your settings:** the App's language and its light or dark theme.
- **Rating prompt:** the App counts how many times it was opened and whether it has already asked you to rate it, so it asks only once.
- This data is stored only in the App's private storage on your device.
- **Android device backup:** depending on your device settings, Android may include this data in your device's own backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

We do not have access to this data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files or any account information, and it needs no special permission.

## 3. Advertising

- The App displays a banner ad provided by **Google AdMob**.
- AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and information about the ads shown and clicked, as an independent controller under Google's policies.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where else required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App's menu (⋮) > **Privacy options** (shown where this choice applies). If you do not consent, the App keeps working normally, and any ads you see are not personalized.
- On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- **Your favorites, the order of the scales and your settings are never shared with AdMob or used to select ads.**
- More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Analytics and crash reports

- We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the App is used, and **Firebase Crashlytics** to receive reports about crashes and errors.
- These services process an App instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from the IP address (country and region), and usage events.
- Usage events describe what is used in the App, never anything you type: for example, "the text about the French scale was opened", "the Brazilian scale was marked as a favorite", "the scales were reordered", "the language was changed to Spanish", "the theme was changed to dark", "the App was shared".
- Analytics **does not use the advertising ID**.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX), or your consent where the law requires it.
- **Retention:** analytics data is kept for at most 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days.

## 5. Links that leave the App

The menu can open the App's page on Google Play (to rate it), Google Play's rating window inside the App, Android's share sheet (to share a link to the App) and this policy in your browser. The page about each scale links to its source (for example, Wikipedia), which opens in your browser. What happens there is governed by Google Play, by the app you choose to share with, by the website you visit and by your browser, under their own policies. The App does not receive anything back; Google Play does not tell us whether or how you rated the App.

## 6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may also disclose data when required by law, but only to the extent strictly required.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to be informed about with whom data is shared.

- **European Union / EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR):** you may also complain to your local data protection authority.
- **Brazil (LGPD, art. 18):** you may also complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).
- **United States (California and other states with consumer privacy laws):** you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Categories collected: identifiers (App instance ID, advertising ID, IP address), internet or electronic activity (App usage events) and approximate geolocation (from IP).

The App has no account, so we cannot link the analytics or crash data to you by your name or email. To exercise a right, email **etcho.apps@gmail.com**; we will answer within the period required by law. For ads, you can also use the choices in Section 3.

## 8. Age

The App is a reference tool for climbers, for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us.

## 9. Security

The App sends data only to the Google services above, over encrypted connections (TLS). Your favorites, the order of the scales and your settings never leave your device.

When personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards offered by Google in its data processing terms, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the mechanisms of art. 33 of the LGPD.
